About Dandy Online

Dandy Online brings John Palevich's legendary Atari 800 roguelike dungeon crawl into modern web browsers with sub-frame deterministic rollback netcode over WebRTC DataChannels. No backend game servers are required — game states and input streams synchronize peer-to-peer with zero lag, instant prediction, and automatic frame rollback recovery.

# Plan: Milestone 5 — Adversarial Hardening & Final Audit

## Objectives
1. **Adversarial Coverage Hardening (Tier 5)**: Inverted loop where 2 Challengers analyze the codebase for gaps, generate adversarial tests, Worker integrates and fixes, and Reviewers verify. Repeat until zero gaps remain.
2. **Final Forensic Audit**: Run Forensic Auditor to verify ROM size, flat 32KB layout, correctness, and lack of cheating.
3. **Compile Final Metrics**: Compile and record raw vs. compressed sizes, ROM size, active segment size, and test pass rate.
